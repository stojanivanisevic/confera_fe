## 0.3.0 – 2026-10-18

The web application of release 0.3.0 (forms, embed and intake), against `confera_be` 0.3.0 (`release/0.3.0`, `6521310`; the vendored contract and its lock name that commit, and the contract is the one of `93938dd` with the version 0.3.0). Design systems 2.5.0 and 2.5.1 are part of it.

### Added
- **The form renderer (M3-FE-01).** `@confera/form-renderer` renders the published definition of a form (`contracts/form-definition.schema.json` of `confera_be`, schema 1, vendored by `pnpm api:sync`) with the design system: texts in both languages, sections as cards, the block of authors, fields shown on a condition (the paper, the employer's billing data), radios, lists, long texts with their word count, files, the consent. The rule interpreter of the browser is the API's and passes every shared vector of `contracts/form-rule-vectors.json` (answers, word count, URL rule, address rule, link syntax, proof-of-work); a refusal of the API (400 `validation_failed`, `answers.<path>`) comes back to the same field in the same words. Errors are listed in the order of the form, name their field and, in the block, their author (UX-M1-08); the summary takes the focus after every failed Send and its items lead to their fields.
- **Authors (FR-FORM-03; UX-M1-09, 10; D4, D7).** After "Add an author" the focus is in the new author's first field and it is announced; at the form's limit (10 unless configured) the button goes and a line says the limit; removing an author who holds data asks first, inside the form (no dialog in a form, ADR-07); when the first author goes the form says who now receives the emails; the phone and the autofill hints belong to the first author only (UX-M1-23).
- **The public pages (M3-FE-02).** `/f/<form>` and `/embed/<form>` read the form from the API on the server (no cache) and fail closed; the frame's site list comes from `/public/v1/forms/<slug>/frame-sites` (on `prod` a loopback entry closes the frame, M1-ISS-39, M2-ISS-21). The language a form opens in is the snippet's `?lang=`, else the browser's (Serbian also for Croatian, Bosnian and Montenegrin), else English (D2); it switches in place and keeps every value; the page title follows it ("Prijava apstrakta · ITEMA 2026", UX-M1-19). Closed and not-yet-open forms say so with their deadline in the conference's zone (FR-DL-02); the thank-you has the reference and the address the confirmation goes to (UX-M1-18). The trap field, the single-use token fetched by the page, the proof-of-work when the API asks for it and one Idempotency-Key per form instance (FR-FORM-14, ADR-08). The content security policy is enforced on the public pages (ADR-28). The frame paints no ground of its own on the site and has an inner gutter (UI-M1-18); it shows no heading of its own (D5).
- **The draft of the tab (FR-FORM-18; D3).** Every field but files and the consent, the authors and the language are kept in the tab with the idempotency key, and come back after a reload; where storage is refused the tab asks before it closes while something is typed (UX-M1-13).
- **Files (M3-FE-04).** One file per field, uploaded at once with its progress; refused before the upload when its type or size is outside the field's rule, and by the API by content, with a message in the form's language that states the limit (20 MB is taken, one byte more is not); a `.docx` with macros says how to save it without them.
- **The snippet (M3-FE-03; D1, D2, D5).** It carries the site's language on the frame and the link; the link "Form not showing? Open it on its own page." stands above the frame, in the same tab; the inline lines bring the summary or the thank-you into the site's window after Send, within the Security reviewer's limits (origin and sender checked, only that frame, vertical, only when out of view, `top` clamped to the frame's height, at most once a second). The admin shows it per form, for English, Serbian or the visitor's browser, with the listed sites.
- **Catalogues and formats (M3-FE-07).** The fixed texts of the public form in English and Serbian (Latin script, full diacritics, *Vi* throughout) with a parity check; dates, times, numbers, amounts and sizes in the conference's time zone, with goldens around midnight and both clock changes.
- **Form configuration (M3-FE-05).** Forms per conference (a new abstract form with a click), the texts in both languages with the one link syntax, the most authors and their notice, every field of the catalogue in a drawer (shown, required, label, help, limits, file types and size, options), the preview of the draft through the renderer of the sites, the versions and Publish, the form's send breaker. A draft the server refuses says what is wrong, field by field.
- **Deadlines, submissions (M3-FE-06).** The three deadlines of a conference with their history (FR-DL-03); the submissions table, a page at a time, narrowed by the conference switch, rows opened by keyboard (M1-ISS-10); one submission with its data under the labels of its form version, the authors, the files with their scan state (downloaded only when clean; an old `.doc` with macros flagged), the consent with its time and version, the history and the emails.
- **Held emails (M3-FE-08).** The list with why each email waits, release of the selected or of all, the organisation's breaker, the screen's word that a deadline day can trip the breaker and one click releases, and the alert of the shell while emails are held.
- **The API stub** answers the public form API (`e2e/servers/api-stub-public.mjs`, from `public-forms.json`, which `make-public-forms.mjs` writes from the vendored contract) and the admin API of 0.3.0 (`api-stub-part-c.mjs`).
- **Design system 2.5.0** (UX-M1-21, 23; from 2.4.1): the language switch of a form names English and Srpski each in its own language (`lang` on the pill, WCAG 3.1.2); the " *" of a required file field is not read out.
- **Tests:** M3-QA-01 against the real API (the seeded form `e2eprobnaforma01` in the snippet on the hostile site, three engines, 1440 and 375 px; Send pressed twice gives one submission and one email); ST-36 (a site taken off the list is blocked at the next load of the frame page); ST-42 (markup and scripts in configured texts stay text); `qa-m3-real-api-admin.spec.ts` opens the seeded form's admin screen and pastes its snippet into the harness site.

### Fixed
- **Q3-01:** the page never sends sooner than the API's fill time (3 s) after it received its token, so a thank-you always stands for a stored submission: a restored draft sent at once, or a Send whose token was refused or had expired, waits out the rest ("Sending…"). A refused token (403 `form_token_invalid`, 409 `form_token_used`, 403 `proof_of_work_failed`) is renewed with `previousFormToken` (the renewal keeps the fill start and the uploaded files), its challenge solved, and the submit sent once more: once per Send. The public form carries the organisation's contact email (M3-ISS-05).
- **Q3-08:** the proof-of-work asks for its hashes a round at a time and leaves the page its turn every 12 ms; a Send that still waits for it says "Checking that you are not a robot…" ("Proveravamo da niste robot…") after a second, and the fields stay editable.
- **Q3-12 (in part):** on a site that zooms its content with CSS `zoom`, the whole form is inside the frame in WebKit too (it was 1,135 px short): WebKit lays the frame's page out in zoomed pixels but measures it in unzoomed ones, so the frame reads the factor from its viewport and tells heights multiplied by it. The snippet is unchanged; a snippet already pasted needs nothing. The embedded page is as tall as its form (no `min-height: 100vh`). Not fixable here: WebKit zooms the frame's layout viewport as well, so there the frame keeps blank room to scroll below the form and clips its right fifth (M3-ISS-09).
- **Q3-09:** against a real API the specs on the stub's forms are left out by name (`STUB_FORM_SPECS`), and the form tests of `csp`, `pages` and `ds-2-3-0` are skipped.
- **Q3-10:** `qa-real-api-flows.spec.ts` checks the used invitation link by the page's heading ("This link does not work any more") and its error alert.
- **UX3-01:** a file chosen or uploaded is said in the form's polite live region; the reason of a refusal is an alert at the file and is heard once, from there. **Design system 2.5.0 → 2.5.1:** FileDropzone gives the reason of a refused file `role="alert"`.
- **The Blank-slate verification of 0.3.0:** the real-API specs that wait for an email add their own default mail server (`e2e/support/mail-server.ts`), so they no longer depend on `qa-real-api-flows` running first; `api:check` generates the types of a document once per process, and the lint-fixtures hook and the contract tests have limits for a loaded machine; the README gives the loaded-machine times of `check` and the browser run, says how to try the embedded form by hand, and that the real-API mail tests need a mail server. Run 2: the M3-QA-01 mail wait is 120 s (the outbox worker's 5 s and 30 a minute per server); the Firefox table test of `ds-2-4-0` has 150 s; the README names the API's 10 submits an hour per client; `snippet.html` is ignored. Run 2b: the clipboard of Windows gives its lines back with CR LF, which `qa-m3-real-api-admin.spec.ts` now reads as LF.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-16): the admin, conferences, mail and the template-editor spike

The web application of release 0.2.0, against `confera_be` 0.2.0 (`release/0.2.0`, `91f6085`; the vendored contract and its lock name that commit). Design systems 2.3.0, 2.4.0 and 2.4.1 are part of it.

### Added
- **The admin screens of 0.2.0 part A (M2-FE-01, M2-FE-02).** The admin shell (navigation, the conference switch as a placeholder, the person and sign-out, the guard of the session with the `next` rule of ST-26); sign-in, forgot password, the pages of the invitation, reset and email-change links with the **live password checklist** (G2-25; the API's rule set vendored with its vectors and run in the browser, so nothing is sent while the password is typed, ST-62); my account; users (list, search, filters, invitation, rename, role, deactivate, reactivate, remove, resend, end sessions, each with the password asked again; the last-admin rule's refusal shown); the organisation profile (issuer data; payment details behind the password-again dialog; the 14-day notice); the audit log. The texts follow the design system's Voice, and the admin's connection card says "Confera cannot reach its server" with a button to try again (UX-M1-14; the support contact is the profile's contact email until decision D8).
- **CSRF plumbing** (ADR-03): `@confera/api-client` gives a signed-in browser two clients that carry the session's `X-CSRF-Token` on every changing request and mark a background refetch with `X-Confera-Background`.
- **The password rule set is vendored** with the API contract (`pnpm api:sync` takes `contracts/password-rules.json` of `confera_be` too; the lock holds it), and every shared vector gives the same four answers here.
- **The API stub of the browser tests** answers the admin API of 0.2.0 part A as the contract describes it, one universe per worker; `e2e/admin.spec.ts` and `e2e/admin-axe.spec.ts` hold the screens in three engines at 375 and 1440 px.

- **The screens of 0.2.0 part B (M2-FE-03, M2-FE-04).** The conference switch, kept across screens and a reload; conferences (a new draft, the settings with the listed sites checked line by line and the time zone, open, close, archive, a copy); mail servers (the password write-only, the password asked again, a new target clears the stored password, a server in use, the test message); the mail log with a stored body only in the sandboxed frame (ST-28); the switch for the mail log's content in the organisation profile. The navigation shows the entries the person's permissions allow.
- **The template editor, the spike of ADR-12 (M2-FE-05).** `@confera/template-editor`: ProseMirror with the closed template format as its schema, a toolbar of design-system controls, a placeholder as one indivisible object, a check and a preview as the server's (`@confera/api-client` checks and renders the format; `template-vectors.json` is vendored like the password rules and every vector passes; the 14 seeded templates load and save unchanged). No save until the API has a write route (0.4.0).

- **The fixes of the 0.2.0 UX and UI review** (the five highs and most mediums: UXUI-M2-01 to 10, 12 to 17, 19, 21 to 23; the lows 30, 33, 35, 38; and from its re-check, 41 and 42). Right after an invitation the toast and the page say "saved" and the page says "sent" once the API does (42). An invitation says what became of it (`invitationDelivery`: waiting for a mail server, failed, on its way), and the invite dialog says when no mail server is set up; an ended session offers "Sign in in a new tab" and Continue without losing the page; the conference switch shows on the mail log and the templates only, is not set by creating a conference, and a conference lists the organisation's templates; the home lists what to do first; the audit log names objects and fields in words; screens behind sign-in without a session cookie are a server redirect to sign-in; the shell stays when the API does not answer; the checklist starts with nothing ticked; forms are checked before the password is asked; the last admin's actions say why they are off; refusals take the focus; a refused mail-server target is said at its field.
- **Design system 2.4.0** (the design-system findings of the review: UXUI-M2-03, 04, 22, 25, 26, 27, 35; M2-ISS-01, 07, 08): a DataTable is a card per row below 600px of its own width and its scroll area takes the focus while it scrolls; the phone menu closes when a link is chosen and the content takes the focus; a skip link; toggles that are on draw the ring of a chosen pill; icons for the editor's toolbar; Alert breaks long words; field messages of two lines do not touch. ## 0.1.0 (2026-10-14): foundations, risk spikes and design system v2

### Added
- **Workspace and toolchain (M1-FE-01).** pnpm 11.28.2 workspace on Node 22 with one Next 16.4 application and the packages `ui`, `form-renderer`, `embed-loader` and `api-client`; TypeScript 6.0.3 strict; `pnpm check`. Supply-chain rules: frozen lockfile, exact versions, a 3-day minimum release age, build scripts by name, strict engines and peers, a vulnerability audit that fails on high, a secret scan. Lint rules with planted violations: no second UI kit, no colour literal, raw markup only in the one reviewed component, the environment only in the configuration module, no server actions. Framework settings: telemetry off, no version header, no public source map, image optimiser off; the production build is checked against the declared routes.
- **Design system 2.0.0 (M1-FE-03, M1-FE-06).** `tokens.css`; the contrast tokens of ADR-22 (`on-brand`, `brand-orange-strong`, `danger-hover`, a darker `success-strong`), `brand-orange` unchanged; self-hosted Manrope and Inter; complete types; 12 icons; translatable labels and `LabelsProvider`; the accessibility fixes; 16px controls on touch devices; version, changelog and checksums. See `design-system/CHANGELOG.md`.
- **The adapter (M1-FE-02).** `@confera/ui` wraps the design-system script, unchanged, so that it renders on the server and hydrates under React 19; `pnpm ds:build`, `pnpm ds:check`, `pnpm ds:visual`, `pnpm ds:snapshot`; the component gallery (test builds only).
- **The embed (M1-FE-04).** The snippet of decision G2-17 (a), the frame page `/embed/[form]`, the hosted page `/f/[form]`, the frame's height reporter, and the spike form with a draft storage that survives a browser that refuses storage.
- **API client (M1-FE-05).** The contract of `confera_be` vendored with a lock, types generated on TypeScript 6.0.3, `pnpm api:sync` and `pnpm api:check`; the admin home asks `GET /api/v1/meta` and stops on another major version.
- **Security headers and host separation (M1-FE-07).** Headers by kind of page in one place; `frame-ancestors 'none'` as the default and the site list only on the frame page; a nonce per response and `no-store` on every page; the baseline policy report-only; admin and public routes each answered only on their own host name; `/api/v1` and `/public/v1` forwarded with the `Host` header kept; the application refuses to start on a bad configuration.
- **Tests.** Unit tests (Vitest), browser tests on Chromium, Firefox and WebKit (Playwright) with a hostile harness site, and accessibility scans (axe, WCAG 2.1 A and AA). Chromium starts on an isolated profile, so that on Windows it never tries to log on to the account; a guard reads the account's bad-password count around a run and switches the browser tests off on the machine when it rises; Playwright's own windows are refused (README, "Browser tests").
- **Test engineering (M1-QA-01, M1-QA-03).** `pnpm test:supply-chain`, the last step of `pnpm check`: planted fake secrets, a vulnerable package and an unlisted build script must each be caught; the install settings held by a test; the bans of the lint held on the syntax tree in any spelling; browser probes at 320 px and under zoom, with a resizing or forging conference site, failing storage, the keyboard only and reduced motion; odd `Host` headers.
- **Cross-repo smoke test (M1-QA-02).** With `CONFERA_E2E_API_ORIGIN=http://127.0.0.1:3001` the browser tests run in front of a real `confera_be` that was started by its documented commands, and the project `real-api` asks what only the real API can answer (README, section 6). The variable takes an address of this machine only; the run first asks whether the API is ready.

### Fixed
- **The secret scan catches a secret that has no format (F1).** A literal value bound to a name like a secret fails `pnpm secrets`, in any syntax where a name gets a value. The rule and the scanner are those of `confera_be` (scanner 1.0.0): `scripts/secret-assignments/` is copied from there unchanged with its manifest (`pnpm secrets:sync`); its self-test, with both corpora and the manifest, runs in the unit tests, and a lock holds the version and the manifest, compared with the checkout of `confera_be` next to this one when there is one. This repository follows the rule: two labels of the design system are renamed (design system 2.1.0), and so is a rule of the style lint (`unknown-custom-property`).
- **The focus stays in a Repeater when a row is removed (F2, WCAG 2.4.3).** Design system 2.0.1; see `design-system/CHANGELOG.md`.
- **Text typed before the form is attached is kept (F3).** The fields of a form are plain controls that the page reads through a store (`createFormValueStore`, `useFormValues`, `useFormRef`); no render writes into a control the author has touched, and a draft fills only untouched fields.
- **The account guard also stops a run that is under way.** The "switched off" marker was read only before a run. Every worker now reads it before it starts a browser, of any engine. The README says what the guard cannot see: a successful logon sets Windows' count back to zero.
- **Browser tests far from their limits.** WebKit tests of a few seconds took 40 to 60 s of their 60 s limit as soon as three workers ran at once: the trace recorded a film strip of every frame, WebKit paints at device scale 2 without a graphics card, and the gallery never stops painting. The trace is now recorded without the film strip (a failed test still has its actions with the page as it was, and a picture of the failure); the gallery test that did the work of nine components, and the accessibility tests that scanned three or four pages or states, are one test per kind of behaviour, page or state; a check asked 53 times is asked once. The two files whose tests load or scan the whole gallery run their tests one after the other.
- **`pnpm dev` and `pnpm start` listen on loopback outside prod (E3).** Next listened on every address of the machine, and through its forwarding of `/api/v1` (with the client's `Host`) reached an API that itself listens on loopback only. The address is `LISTEN_HOST`, by default `127.0.0.1` outside `prod` and `0.0.0.0` on `prod`; on `local`, `dev` and `test` anything but a loopback address, and any `-H`/`--hostname`, stops the start. The same rule as in `confera_be`.
- **The two surfaces differ in host name, not only in port or scheme (E4).** A cookie does not tell ports apart, so one name on two ports carried the admin's session to the public pages. The same rule as in `confera_be`.
- **The height of an embedded form follows on sites that run scripts late or put the frame in later (E7).** The inline lines looked the frame up once, when they ran. They now look it up when a message arrives, by the window that sent it, and make a frame that already shows its page one pixel taller so that it tells its height again; until the page has once followed a height the frame told, the frame answers a change of its size by telling its height again. A snippet pasted before this change keeps the old behaviour until it is pasted again.
- **A field may have any name (E9).** The values of a form are kept in an object without a prototype, so a field named `__proto__`, `constructor` or `toString` keeps its answer.
- **No HTML response without its header set (ST-12).** A path under `/_next/` that names no file was answered by the framework with the "not found" page and none of the security headers: the proxy left all of `/_next/` out. It now leaves out only `/_next/static/`, whose files get `nosniff`; every other path, the error page of a page that fails included, gets the header set of its kind.
- **The moment after Send (UX-M1-01 to 04, 07; UI-M1-06, 11).** Every rule of the form is checked on Send, the word limit at once, and the e-mail address of author 1 is required; a failed Send moves the focus to the error summary, whose items are links to their fields; a successful Send moves it to the thank-you; inside a frame the form asks the site to bring that part into view (the snippet does not act on it before decision D1). The consent is marked as required and says what is wrong at the box. Texts: "First and last name of author {n}" / "Ime i prezime autora {n}"; the draft note says what is kept, the title and the abstract (UX-M1-05).
- **UX-M1-08, in part.** The summary has one item for each author without a name, and names the author. Its title still says "Please correct the marked fields": M1-ISS-31.
- **Nothing typed goes into an address (UX-M1-03).** Send is disabled until the form is attached; the form has `method="post"` and its own POST path, which answers 204; a `<noscript>` line where scripts never run.
- **A page answers GET and HEAD only (SEC-M1-01).** Every other method, and any request with `Next-Action`, gets 405 before the framework; the paths forwarded to the API keep every method.
- **Design system 2.2.0** (UI-M1-01 to 03, 05 to 07, 09, 20; UI-M1-04, 10, 15 and 16 in part): keyboard focus in menus and lists, Menu and the AppShell drawer give the focus back, Tooltip hides on Escape and can be hovered, selected states with an inner ring, success borders in `success-strong`, a required Checkbox with its message, a horizontal Stepper that fits 375 px. See `design-system/CHANGELOG.md`. Its 2.2.0 entry says more of UI-M1-15 and 16 than was done, and the design system's README says that every focusable part shows the blue outline; both are corrected in the next design-system release.
  - **UI-M1-10, in part.** The remove button of a Tag and the clear button of SearchField are 24 x 24 px. Nothing grows on a touch screen yet (no 44 px targets), the rows of checkboxes and radios are still 18 px high, and the form's add and remove author buttons and its consent row are as small as before: M1-ISS-20.
  - **UI-M1-15, in part.** The same two buttons show the system's blue focus outline. The page-size select of Pagination, the links of Breadcrumbs and the step button of Stepper still show the browser's own ring: M1-ISS-21.
  - **UI-M1-16, in part.** The vertical Stepper keeps its connectors at every width. The remove button of a FileDropzone row that shows progress still wraps, the last row of PermissionMatrix still has a border under its first column, and DateField still shows two calendar icons in Chromium: M1-ISS-22.
  - **UI-M1-04, in part.** A Tooltip's bubble is not yet kept inside the window: near the left edge at 375 px it is cut off.
