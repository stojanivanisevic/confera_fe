# Logos

The UdEkoM Balkans mark is a ring of figures around an orange centre, drawn in `brand-orange` with `ink` (light grounds) or white (dark grounds).

- `udekom-mark.png`: the mark in orange and ink, for light grounds (44px).
- `udekom-mark-on-dark.png`: the mark in orange and white, for `background-dark` (101px, footer).
- `udekom-logo-horizontal-on-dark.png`: the mark with the "UDEKOM BALKAN" wordmark in white, for dark grounds (120x44).

These are PNG renders at their drawn size from the Figma file. Use them only at that size or smaller. Vector masters are still to come (see the README, Not synced). Keep clear space of at least half the mark's height on every side, and never recolour or stretch the mark.
